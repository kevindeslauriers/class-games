## MONEY WORDS
1 | The original amount of money invested or borrowed | PRINCIPAL / THE PRINCIPAL

3 | Interest earned on the principal and on interest already earned | COMPOUND INTEREST | SIMPLE INTEREST; PRINCIPAL; AMORTIZATION
4 | The total of principal plus interest at the end | AMOUNT / FUTURE VALUE / ACCUMULATED AMOUNT
