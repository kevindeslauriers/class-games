## WHAT A LOG IS
1 | log₂ 8 asks: 2 to what power gives 8? What is the answer? | = 3 ~0
2 | Write 10³ = 1000 in logarithmic form | log 1000 = 3 | log 3 = 1000; log₃ 1000 = 10; log 10 = 3
3 | Write log₅ 25 = 2 in exponential form | 5² = 25 | 2⁵ = 25; 25² = 5; 5²⁵ = 2
4 | A logarithm with base 10 is called a ___ logarithm | COMMON
5 | A logarithm with base e is called the ___ logarithm, written ln | NATURAL
