## UP OR DOWN?
1 | y = 3(2)^x shows exponential ___ | GROWTH | DECAY; LINEAR CHANGE; NO CHANGE
2 | y = 100(0.8)^x shows exponential ___ | DECAY
3 | In y = a(b)^x, the graph shows decay when b is this | BETWEEN 0 AND 1 | GREATER THAN 1; NEGATIVE; EQUAL TO 1
4 | A population grows 5% a year. What is the growth factor b? | = 1.05 ~0
5 | A car loses 15% of its value each year. What is the decay factor b? | = 0.85 ~0

## DECAY PROBLEMS

5 | Coffee cools so the temperature difference drops 10% every minute. A 60° difference becomes what after 3 minutes, to 1 decimal place? | = 43.7 ~0.5%

## TRANSFORMING EXPONENTIALS
1 | y = 2^x + 3 has this horizontal asymptote | y = 3 | y = 0; y = 2; x = 3
2 | y = 2^(x − 4) is y = 2^x shifted this way | RIGHT 4 | LEFT 4; UP 4; DOWN 4
3 | y = −2^x is y = 2^x reflected in this | THE x-AXIS | THE y-AXIS; y = x; THE LINE y = 1
4 | What is the y-intercept of y = 3(2)^x − 1? | = 2 ~0
5 | y = 2^(−x) is the same as this | y = (½)^x | y = −2^x; y = 2x; y = −½x

3 | In an exponential relation, the ratio of consecutive y-values is this | CONSTANT | ZERO; INCREASING; ALWAYS 2
