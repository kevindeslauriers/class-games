## HOW SPREAD OUT
1 | The most common measure of how far data values are from the mean | STANDARD DEVIATION / SD / STANDARD DEVIATIONS
2 | The variance is this | THE STANDARD DEVIATION SQUARED | THE SQUARE ROOT OF THE STANDARD DEVIATION; THE RANGE ÷ 2; THE MEAN SQUARED
3 | A population has a variance of 36. What is its standard deviation? | = 6 ~0
4 | The data 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9 have a mean of 5. What is their population standard deviation? | = 2 ~0
5 | Every value in a data set is increased by 10. What happens to the standard deviation? | IT STAYS THE SAME | IT INCREASES BY 10; IT DOUBLES; IT INCREASES BY 100

## Z-SCORES
1 | A z-score measures how many of these a value is from the mean | STANDARD DEVIATIONS | RANGES; PERCENTILES; QUARTILES
2 | The mean is 70 and the standard deviation is 5. What is the z-score of 80? | = 2 ~0
3 | The mean is 70 and the standard deviation is 5. What is the z-score of 62.5? | = -1.5 ~0
4 | A value has a z-score of 0. Where is it? | AT THE MEAN | AT THE MINIMUM; ONE STANDARD DEVIATION ABOVE; AT Q1
5 | Ana scored 80 on a test with mean 70 and SD 5. Ben scored 85 on a test with mean 75 and SD 10. Who did better relative to their class? | ANA | BEN; THEY DID EQUALLY WELL; IT CAN'T BE TOLD

## PERCENTILES AND QUARTILES

3 | Being in the 90th percentile means this | YOU SCORED AS WELL AS OR BETTER THAN 90% OF THE GROUP | YOU GOT 90% ON THE TEST; 90 PEOPLE SCORED BELOW YOU; YOU ARE IN THE TOP 90%
4 | Dividing ordered data into 10 equal parts gives these | DECILES / DECILE
5 | In a class of 25, a student's mark is higher than 20 others' marks. To the nearest whole number, what percentile is that? | = 80 ~0

## UNUSUAL VALUES
1 | A value far from the rest of the data | OUTLIER / AN OUTLIER
2 | The data are 10, 12, 13, 14, 60. Removing the 60 does this to the mean | LOWERS IT A LOT | RAISES IT; LEAVES IT THE SAME; LOWERS IT SLIGHTLY
3 | Q1 = 10 and Q3 = 18. Using the 1.5 × IQR rule, values below what number are outliers? | = -2 ~0
4 | Q1 = 10 and Q3 = 18. Using the 1.5 × IQR rule, values above what number are outliers? | = 30 ~0
5 | Using z-scores, a value is often called an outlier if its z-score is beyond this | ±3 | ±0.5; ±1; ±1.5
// "±3" is the usual rule of thumb; some texts use ±2.

## CHANGING THE DATA

2 | Every value is doubled. What happens to the standard deviation? | IT DOUBLES | IT STAYS THE SAME; IT IS SQUARED; IT HALVES
3 | The mean of 4 values is 12. A fifth value of 22 is added. What is the new mean? | = 14 ~0
4 | Every value is multiplied by 3. The variance was 4. What is the new variance? | = 36 ~0
5 | A teacher adds 5 marks to every test. The median was 68. What is the new median? | = 73 ~0
