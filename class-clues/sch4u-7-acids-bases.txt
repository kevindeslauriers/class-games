## BRØNSTED–LOWRY
1 | A Brønsted–Lowry acid is a proton ___ | DONOR | ACCEPTOR; DESTROYER; CARRIER
2 | A Brønsted–Lowry base is a proton ___ | ACCEPTOR
3 | What is the conjugate base of HCl? | Cl⁻ | H⁺; H2Cl⁺; OH⁻
4 | What is the conjugate acid of NH3? | NH4⁺ | NH2⁻; H3O⁺; N2
5 | A substance that can act as an acid or a base, like water | AMPHOTERIC / AMPHIPROTIC

## pH AND pOH
1 | pH = −log of this | [H3O⁺] (OR [H⁺]) | [OH⁻]; Kw; Ka

5 | What is [H⁺] at pH 3.5, in mol/L? | = 3.16e-4 ~1%

## WEAK ACIDS

2 | The equilibrium constant for a weak acid ionizing | Ka / ACID DISSOCIATION CONSTANT / ACID IONIZATION CONSTANT

5 | A 0.10 mol/L weak acid has pH 3.0. What is its Ka? | = 1.0e-5 ~2%

## RESISTING CHANGE
