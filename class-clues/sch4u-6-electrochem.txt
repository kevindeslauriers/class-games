## REDOX BASICS
1 | Losing electrons is called this | OXIDATION | REDUCTION; NEUTRALIZATION; IONIZATION
2 | Gaining electrons is called this | REDUCTION
3 | A memory aid for redox: "LEO the lion says ___" | GER
4 | The substance that gets reduced is called the ___ agent | OXIDIZING | REDUCING; NEUTRALIZING; CATALYTIC
5 | In Zn + Cu²⁺ → Zn²⁺ + Cu, which species is oxidized? | Zn | Cu²⁺; Zn²⁺; Cu

## WHICH IS STRONGER?

2 | Of Zn²⁺, Fe²⁺, Cu²⁺ and Ag⁺, which is the strongest oxidizing agent? | Ag⁺ | Zn²⁺; Fe²⁺; Cu²⁺
3 | Of Zn, Fe, Cu and Ag, which is the strongest reducing agent? | Zn | Fe; Cu; Ag
4 | Will copper metal react with Zn²⁺ ions? | NO | YES; ONLY WHEN HEATED; ONLY IN A SALT BRIDGE
5 | Will zinc metal react with Cu²⁺ ions? | YES | NO; ONLY WITH A CATALYST; ONLY IN BASE
