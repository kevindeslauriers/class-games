## THE NORMAL CURVE
1 | A normal distribution has this shape | BELL-SHAPED AND SYMMETRIC | SKEWED RIGHT; FLAT (UNIFORM); TWO-PEAKED
2 | In a normal distribution, the mean, median and mode are this | ALL EQUAL | ALL DIFFERENT; MEAN > MEDIAN > MODE; MODE > MEDIAN > MEAN
3 | The total area under a normal curve equals this | = 1 ~0
4 | A larger standard deviation makes a normal curve do this | WIDER AND FLATTER | TALLER AND NARROWER; SHIFT RIGHT; SKEW LEFT
5 | Changing only the mean of a normal distribution does this to the curve | SHIFTS IT LEFT OR RIGHT | MAKES IT WIDER; MAKES IT TALLER; SKEWS IT

## NORMAL APPROXIMATION
1 | A binomial distribution can be approximated by a normal one when np and nq are both greater than this | = 5 ~0
2 | For a binomial distribution with n = 100 and p = 0.5, what is the mean of the normal approximation? | = 50 ~0
3 | For n = 100 and p = 0.5, what is the standard deviation, √(npq)? | = 5 ~0
4 | Using a normal curve for a discrete count, P(X ≥ 60) becomes P(X > 59.5). This adjustment is called the ___ correction | CONTINUITY
5 | A coin is tossed 100 times. Using the normal approximation with continuity correction, what is P(at least 60 heads), to 3 decimal places? | = 0.029 ~5%

## HOW SURE ARE WE?
1 | A range of values likely to contain the true population value is called a ___ interval | CONFIDENCE
2 | For a 95% confidence interval, the z-value used is this | 1.96 | 1.28; 1.645; 2.58
3 | A poll says 52% support a plan, accurate to within 3 percentage points, 19 times out of 20. What confidence level is that? | 95% | 90%; 99%; 68%
4 | 42 of the next 350 cars on the DVP are blue. What is the sample proportion of blue cars? | = 0.12 ~0.5%
5 | For p̂ = 0.12 and n = 350, the 95% margin of error is 1.96 √(p̂(1 − p̂) ÷ n). What is it, to 3 decimal places? | = 0.034 ~2%

## PLUS OR MINUS
1 | The "plus or minus" part of a poll result is called the ___ of error | MARGIN
2 | Increasing the sample size does this to the margin of error | DECREASES IT | INCREASES IT; LEAVES IT UNCHANGED; DOUBLES IT
3 | Raising the confidence level from 90% to 99% does this to the interval | MAKES IT WIDER | MAKES IT NARROWER; LEAVES IT THE SAME; SHIFTS IT RIGHT
4 | A poll finds 40% support with a margin of error of 4%. What is the upper end of the interval, in percent? | = 44 % ~0
5 | To cut the margin of error in half, the sample size must be multiplied by this | = 4 ~0
