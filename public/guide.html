Class Board: Teacher Guide

A quiz board for the whole class. Your computer runs the projector, and every student answers every clue on their own laptop or phone, in table-group teams.

Two addresses

You (teacher, on the projector)class-games.onrender.com/class
Studentsclass-games.onrender.com

Run a game in 5 steps

  1. Open the teacher page on the projector

    Go to /class on your computer and click Full screen. Click anywhere once so the sounds can play. A game code and a QR code appear.

  2. Students join

    They scan the QR code or go to the student address, type the code and their first name, then tap their team (their table group). Names appear under each team as they join.

  3. Choose the settings

    Pick the course and the units the categories should come from, then the options (see below). Click Shuffle into even teams if you'd rather not use table groups.

  4. Play

    Click Start the game. A team picks a clue (or you do), everyone answers, and the results show on the board. Click Back to the board after each clue.

  5. Download the report

    At the end, click Download the report (CSV). It opens in Excel or Google Sheets. Nothing is saved on the server, so download it before you leave.

Settings in the lobby

Course
Grade 9 Science (the default), Grade 9 Geography, Grade 11 Functions and Applications, Grade 12 Advanced Functions, Grade 12 Chemistry, Grade 12 Data Management or AP Microeconomics.
Units
Tick the units to draw categories from. One unit is enough for two full boards.
Teams
2 to 8. Students on a team you remove move to the smallest team.
Boards
One board, or two with the second worth double.
Time per clue
20 to 90 seconds. Number answers get 50% more time; the final clue gets double.
Teams pick the clues
On: teams take turns picking on their devices. Off: you click every clue. Teams still take turns for Daily Doubles.
Daily Doubles
Hidden squares. The team whose turn it is agrees on a wager.
Final clue
Every team wagers, then everyone answers one last clue.

Your screen remembers these settings for next time.

How scoring works

  • Students: a right answer earns the clue's value (100 to 500, doubled on the second board). The board shows only the top three students.
  • Teams: the team earns the clue's value times the share of its members who got it right. If 3 of 4 are right on a 400 clue, the team gets 300. So every student's answer counts.
  • Daily Double and final clue: the team wins its wager if at least half of its members are right, and loses it if not.

During a clue

Answers close
When the timer runs out, or as soon as everyone connected has answered.
Close answers now
Ends the clue early.
Pause
Stops the clock, for example to explain a word. Click again to resume.
Skip this clue
Removes it with no points.
Accept
For typed and number answers, the results group what students wrote. If a group is right but was marked wrong, click Accept and it counts for everyone who wrote it (click Undo to take it back).
End round
Ends a board early when you're running out of time.
What students can type. Spelling doesn't have to be perfect, and there's no need for "What is…". For numbers, fractions (3/8), decimals (0.375), scientific notation (3e8 or 3 x 10^8), "x = 3" and multiples of pi (5π/6 or 5pi/6) all work, and units are optional. Multiple choice can be answered with the A to D keys.

Managing the class

  • Click Class list (or a name in the lobby) to move a student to another team, rename them, or remove them.
  • A simple filter blocks rude names. Rename anything that slips through.
  • Grey names have lost their connection. A student who refreshes or reopens the page rejoins automatically with their score.
  • Click New game to play again with the same students.
  • When the next class arrives, click New class (new code) in the lobby so the new group gets a fresh code and an empty class list.

If something goes wrong

ProblemWhat to do
The first load is slowGive it a few seconds; the server may be starting up.
"No class has that code"Check the code on the board. Codes are 5 letters.
A student is stuck on "Reconnecting"Have them refresh the page. They keep their name and score.
The school network blocks the gameNothing to do: the page switches to a slower connection method on its own.
A shared laptop rejoins as the last studentThe student taps Leave the game and joins with their own name.
No soundClick anywhere on the teacher page, and check Sound on.
The teacher page was closedReopen /class on the same computer and browser; you get the same game back.
The game says it has closedA game with nobody connected closes after 3 hours. Start a new one.

Found a clue that's wrong?

Every clue lives in a plain text file, one clue per line, so fixes are quick. Tell Mr. DesLauriers the course, category and value (for example "Geography, Glaciation, 300") and what it should say.
